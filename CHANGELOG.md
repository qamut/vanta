# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-31

First public release, now open source under the MIT license.

- The island: a capsule fused to the top of the screen that expands on hover, click or `Alt+Shift+Space`.
- Now playing for any Windows media session, with synced lyrics from LRCLIB.
- Converter for images, video, audio and PDF, powered by a bundled FFmpeg (pinned build, GPL v3).
- Downloader for video links (yt-dlp) and image galleries (gallery-dl), downloaded on first use.
- Tasks, water tracking with a bottle, VANTA notifications and device alerts.
- Optional browser extension for Chromium browsers and Firefox.
- Installer for the current user, no administrator rights, with silent mode.
